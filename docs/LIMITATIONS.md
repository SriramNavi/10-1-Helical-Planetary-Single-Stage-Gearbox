# Technical limitations and claim boundaries

**No certified torque rating or physically validated operating envelope. No hardware fabrication or testing occurred in this investigation.**

Observed CAD dimensions, analytical predictions, numerical results and unknowns are separate evidence classes. CAD envelope/nominal fit does not prove load capacity. Static linear elasticity does not validate printed PLA strength, creep, fatigue, wear or temperature limits. Stored mates do not establish structural restraints. A numerical peak is not a material allowable or a physically calibrated concentration factor.

The actual duty, coincident torque-speed-duration points, ambient, life, efficiency, complete backlash and stiffness are unknown. No motor/controller/electronics implementation is claimed. The 0.15 N m end load is a hypothetical comparison coordinate, not a rated output.

Actual bearing identity, internal clearance, load factors, moment transfer and race/retainer compliance are unverified. The rigid journal and tie-hole constraints are idealizations. Physical bolt preload, slip, opening, pilot-gap contact and collar/hex load transfer are not closed. Explicit native separated-band preparation failed; no native band solves are claimed.

Candidate geometry retained constrained nominal dimensions but reduced mouth land and flat area. Print realization, physical engagement and tolerance-dependent structural ranking are unverified. Alternative support/combined-load cases are one-mesh screens. Finite refinement passes do not establish asymptotic accuracy or physical validity. The post-mesh minimum-setting discrepancy is documented, not silently repaired.

Historic native hashes differed from the old certificate. The frozen baseline is therefore the authority. The prior approved preservation exception affected one Phase 3 GEN intermediate and one MAS timestamp; native CAD, CWR archives and exported engineering results remained intact. No older manifest was reset. Phase 5's private protection audit covers 2,548 prior durable files.
